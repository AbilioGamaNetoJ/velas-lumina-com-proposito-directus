# Hero Design Enhancement Plan

## 🔍 Context Analysis
- **Sector**: Artisanal Candles (Premium, handmade, comfort).
- **Current State**: The Hero uses a "Safe Split" layout (50/50 text on the left, circular video on the right). While beautiful, it follows a standard, predictable template (the "Modern SaaS Safe Harbor").
- **Goal**: Elevate the design to a more elegant, premium, and unforgettable experience, breaking the "Safe Split" cliché.

## 🎨 Design Commitment (Anti-Safe Harbor)
To make this feel truly premium, we must abandon the predictable 2-column grid. I propose two radical alternatives:

### Opção 1: Immersive Cinematic (Layered Depth)
- **Topological Choice**: The video/GIF becomes a massive background or a full-bleed shape that covers most of the screen. We overlay it with a warm, deep gradient so the text remains readable.
- **Typographic Brutalism**: The text is centered and massive. The user feels "inside" the warm environment immediately.
- **Cliché Liquidation**: Kills the "isolated circle" and the "left text" safe split.
- **Risk Factor**: Text readability requires careful overlay tuning, but the visual impact is breathtaking.

### Opção 2: Editorial Asymmetry (Z-Axis Overlap)
- **Topological Choice**: The video occupies an asymmetrical, sharp-edged or arched pillar on the right (e.g., 40% width but 100% height).
- **Z-Axis Overlap**: The text on the left intentionally overlaps the video area, breaking the grid and creating physical depth.
- **Cliché Liquidation**: Kills the predictable padding and isolated containers.
- **Risk Factor**: Breaking the grid requires precise responsiveness to ensure text doesn't clash poorly on mid-sized screens.
