# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Visitors arriving via organic search, social media, or a direct link — discovering the candle catalog for the first time, often on mobile. The job to be done: find a candle (or gift basket/kit) that matches an intention, mood, or occasion, then message the shop on WhatsApp to buy it or personalize one in the "Laboratório Bespoke" candle builder. There's no account and no online payment — the entire transaction closes inside a WhatsApp conversation with the shopkeeper.

## Product Purpose

Replace a social-media-only presence with a professional, SEO-indexable storefront for handmade, 100% vegetal candles. The site's single job is to turn organic/search traffic into a qualified WhatsApp conversation — every page, section, and CTA exists to reduce friction toward "Enviar Pedido via WhatsApp". Success is measured in WhatsApp messages initiated from the site, not in on-site checkout conversion.

## Brand Personality

Sofisticada, acolhedora, artesanal — an editorial boutique feel (elegant, premium, serif display type) that never loses its handmade warmth. Confidently upscale, never cold; handcrafted, never sloppy-rustic. Voice: warm and inviting in Portuguese copy ("Cada vela carrega um propósito"), never salesy or generic.

## Anti-references

- **Generic marketplace grids** (Amazon/Shopee-style): cold, dense, soulless product tiles with no brand narrative.
- **Cold SaaS/tech minimalism**: dashboard-like starkness, no warmth, no texture.
- **Exaggerated/pretentious luxury editorial**: so over-the-top it reads as inaccessible or out of touch with the real customer messaging from their phone.

## Design Principles

1. **Every path leads to WhatsApp** — minimize friction between discovery and "Enviar Pedido via WhatsApp"; the CTA is never more than a scroll or a click away.
2. **Sophistication with warmth, not chill** — elegant and premium (serif display, considered spacing) without ever reading as cold or corporate.
3. **Show the handmade, not the mass-produced** — texture, imperfection, and craft cues over generic e-commerce polish.
4. **Editorial curation over marketplace density** — a boutique's considered selection, not an infinite grid to search through.
5. **Confidence without pretension** — premium, but never so precious it alienates the real customer buying from their phone.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Standard WCAG AA practices: sufficient color contrast (verify the terracotta-on-warm-white palette against the 4.5:1 body-text bar), full keyboard navigation, descriptive alt text on product imagery. No additional requirement stated beyond that.
